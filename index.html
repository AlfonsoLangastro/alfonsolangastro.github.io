About me

I am a PhD Candidate in the SPS Department at the European University Institute (EUI) in Florence, Italy, under the supervision of Prof. Herman G. Van de Werfhorst.

My research focuses on social mobility dynamics as an empirical way to address normative concerns about equality. I investigate these patterns from a specific occupational sociology angle, studying how polarization in job quality, the rising role of firms, increasing fragmentation in work careers, and AI exposure change what we know about social mobility and, in turn, about equality of opportunity.

Before joining the EUI, I completed an MSc at Bocconi University and an MA at Pompeu Fabra University, combining economics with political philosophy. Before starting my PhD, I also worked for international organizations (UN Women and the OECD) and Italian institutions (the National Research Council, CNR).
